Problem 3: Battle of the Sexes
Husband and wife would like to go on a date-night out and there are only two venues for entertain-
ment that night: an F-1 Race and a K-pop Concert. The wife wants to see the F-1 while the husband
wants to see the Concert. But both of them prefer being together than being alone. Out of love
for each other, they do not explicitly tell each other their own preferences (Bad idea!). The payoff
matrix is shown below where the husband is the Row player and the wife is the Column player.
Payoff matrix:

                                               F −1     Concert
                                   F −1         1, 2      0, 0
                                   Concert      0, 0      2, 1

a) Argue that both ⟨F − 1, F − 1⟩ and ⟨Concert, Concert⟩ are Nash equilibria.

b) Find a mixed-strategy Nash equilibrium.

c) Compute the expected utility at all three equilibria for the husband and for the wife.

d) Compute the utility if each does their preferred activity (mismatch), and the expected utilities
   if both randomize uniformly.

collect $f dollars from E, while if f is even, E collect $f dollars from O. Show the payoff matrix
