Problem 1: Which situations are games?
A game requires multiple decision-makers whose payoffs depend on one another’s actions.
   • OPEC members choosing output: Yes. Each country’s output affects prices and the
     payoffs of other countries.

   • Apple purchasing displays from Samsung: Yes. Since they are competitors, Apple’s
     purchasing decision and Samsung’s pricing, quantity, or supply decision will be
     strategic.

   • Nut and bolt manufacturers choosing standards: Yes. Their payoffs depend on
     whether their standards match.

   • Board setting the CEO’s stock-option plan: No. It becomes a game if there is
     negotiation from both sides.

   • SIA hiring cabin crew: No. It has only one decision-maker that is SIA.

   • Government deciding whether to build a nuclear plant under demand uncertainty:
     No. Demand uncertainty is caused by nature, not another strategic decision-maker.
