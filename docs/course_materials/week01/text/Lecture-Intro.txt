Overview                                                                                                27
Solving Planning Problems
• Planning Problem or Model
    • Appropriate abstraction of states, actions, effects, and goals (and costs and values)
• Planning Algorithm
    • Input: a problem
    • Output: a solution in the form of an action sequence (a plan)
• Planning Solution
    • A plan or path from the initial state(s) to the goal state(s)
           • Any path; OR
           • An optimal path wrt to costs or values
    • A goal state that satisfies certain properties

Making Responsible Decisions
• Main issue:
    • Value gained from accurate results balanced against additional efforts to incorporate responsible
      feature(s) – transparency, privacy, fairness, safety & robustness, human factors, governance & regulation
• Examples:
    • diagnosis recommendations, risk predictions, optimal policies, assistive guidance, recruitment
      interviews, social assistance schemes, university admissions, population censors , consumer behaviors,
      scientific research
• If you are a developer, user, or owner/manager/regulator:
    1.     What is <responsible feature>? Why is it important?
    2.     What can be done to ensure trust (in AI decision systems)?
    3.     What are the techniques and tools available?
    4.     What is the tradeoff between accuracy and <responsible feature> of a trusted agent?
    5.     What are the implications?
    6.     Who should make the decisions? When?
