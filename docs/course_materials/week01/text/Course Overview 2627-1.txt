Required Background for Enrollment
• Discrete Structures
    • Logic, Proofs, Functions, Relations, Recursion, Induction, Combinatorics, Graph Theory
• Probability and Statistics
    • Basic probability theory, random variables, Bayes’ Theorem, probability models,
      information theory, experiment design, hypothesis testing, statistical inference
• Artificial Intelligence
    • Knowledge Representation, Reasoning, Learning, Search
• Linear Algebra and Calculus
    • Matrices, basic matrix operations, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, derivatives,
      maximization and minimization

Course Logistics
• Core Contents
    • All course materials, videos, and announcements available on Canvas.
    • In-person lectures may or may not be recorded. If recorded, videos will be on Canvas.
    • Active participation in Canvas Discussion Forum encouraged (monitored by teaching team).
• Tutorials and Assignments
    • Attempt tutorial questions before class; discuss solutions in class.
    • Tutorial answers submitted in teams of 2–4 students.
    • Assignments include written questions and Python programming tasks.
• Project
    • Guidelines provided in Week 3.
    • Choose a suggested theme or propose your own topic (teams of 4–6 students).
    • One-page proposal due after Recess Week.
Overview                                                                                11
Tutorials
• Objective:
    • Actively participate, understand tutorial solutions deeply, and share helpful examples.
• Team Submission:
    • Form teams of 2–4 students to submit one write-up per tutorial.
    • Clearly include all members' names and student IDs.
• Deadline:
    • Submit via Canvas by Wednesday, 6:00 pm, before the next lecture.
• Grading:
    • Participation-based—marks given for submission, not for correctness.
• Sharing:
    • Selected good submissions will be shared for reference (but are not official model answers).
